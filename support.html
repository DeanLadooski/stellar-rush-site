Common Questions

Can I play offline?

Yes. Stellar Rush saves personal runs locally while offline and syncs eligible leaderboard runs when connection returns.

Why do I need a username?

Usernames are used for leaderboard entries and account recovery across devices.

Can I recover progress on another device?

Yes. Use your username and password to recover your account and synced progress.

Why is the Daily Challenge unavailable offline?

The Daily Challenge uses online validation so each player gets one official attempt on the same daily map.
